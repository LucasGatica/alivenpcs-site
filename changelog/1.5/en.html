A villager's greeting now better matches the relationship you actually have.

  • Someone who already knows you no longer greets you as if it were your first meeting.
Fixes

Personality Manager and multiplayer

Customized characters show up the way they should, and multiplayer syncs the stories of villagers who left the valley more reliably.

  • Compatibility with the AliveNpcs Personality Manager is better.
  • Custom NPC names, identities and pronouns come through more accurately in AI dialogue.
  • With the Personality Manager installed, pressing F7 could open two character sheet screens at once. This is fixed.
  • There are fewer conflicts with other mods that change NPC information.
  • In multiplayer, story events involving NPCs who left or were expelled from the valley sync better.
1.5.0

Us and Them

Jul 24, 2026

1.5.0 is about friendship, resentment, belonging and consequences that reach the whole village.

Experimental

Enmity System

Each relationship can now have an enmity track, separate from friendship. The system is experimental and off by default.

  • Negative interactions can make enmity grow.
  • Enmity hearts show up in black on the Social and Profile screens.
  • Positive interactions help repair the relationship. They grant friendship and remove part of the enmity you built up.
  • The AI gets the enmity context when it evaluates your replies, so reactions and consequences fit a difficult relationship better.
In GMCMEnable Enmity Systemoff by default
Experimental

Expulsion and return arcs

Rare story arcs in which an NPC the village rejects gets arrested, exiled or decides to leave. Later, a return arc can bring them back.

  • Children and your current spouse are always protected.
  • Expulsion can be undone. What happened becomes part of the story memory and shows up in later dialogue.
  • Each save keeps track of its own expulsions.
  • Shops run by expelled NPCs stay accessible, so a story consequence doesn't lock you out of game content.

New

The multiplayer foundation

AliveNpcs now has the groundwork to run in a coordinated way in multiplayer games. Turning it on takes a companion mod, which 1.5.0 announced for release soon.

  • With the companion installed, the host coordinates everything that affects the whole village: gossip, story arcs, social moments, letters, weekly news and mood changes. That way the same content isn't processed several times.
  • Arc progress, completed objectives, relationship changes and notifications can sync between players.
  • Each player keeps their own point of view and gets a personal diary from the interactions they lived through. Shared events stay shared across the farm.
  • Requests to conclude or delete arcs go to the host, so the story stays the same for everyone.
  • The main mod alone doesn't turn multiplayer on. You'll need the compatible companion once it's out.
New

Shops that stay open without their owner

A shop can stay open even when its owner isn't behind the counter.

  • This keeps a shop from closing when its owner marries you, changes their routine or leaves for a while during an expulsion arc.
  • This setting is now saved per save file, and older data migrates on its own.
Improved

A refreshed AliveNpcs Computer

The AliveNpcs Computer arrives on its own at the start of a new game, ready to open the Hub.

  • It comes in three designs you can preview and pick on the village social network screen. Pixel art by @ChaseBlet.
  • The furniture looks better, its interaction area is improved and it ties in better with the mod's menus.
New

Festivals and Beach Episode

AI invitations now work in custom festivals, starting with Beach Episode's Beach Volleyball Championship.

  • NPCs accept or turn down your invitation to team up based on context. Their answer comes in a structured format, which keeps the action reliable.
  • The Parrot Night festival is now recognized and has AI dialogue.
  • The Beach Episode integration is optional. AliveNpcs works normally without it.
Improved

NPCs from other mods

AliveNpcs recognizes eligible NPCs from other mods more reliably, with expanded support for Stardew Valley Expanded.

  • Compatible NPCs take part in dialogue, gossip, letters, story arcs and the other AliveNpcs social systems.
  • To leave an NPC out of the mod, add their name to a comma-separated list.
  • The list of available NPCs respects your settings and the community list of characters whose authors don't allow AI use.
In GMCMDisabled NPCscomma-separated NPC names
Improved

Personality Manager

The Personality Manager integration grew. Each NPC can now have its own customization file, and you can reload them while you play.

  • Customizations can change the displayed name and other character data. The new name shows up correctly in portraits and dialogue screens.
  • The old personality file format still works.
  • When other mods change the same data, AliveNpcs notices, which cuts down on conflicts.
New

Experimental Content selector

Experimental Content (EC) modes now have a visual selector in the mod settings, with a description and a preview image for each mode.

  • The old mode names still work.
  • EC packs can add AI instructions, dialogue actions, scene events, arcs, story memory and NPC data in a more organized way.
Improved

Writing and reading dialogue

The fields where you type replies and actions now take multiple lines, and dialogue got longer.

  • You can click to place the cursor and use the navigation keys to move through the text. Editing long replies is more comfortable.
  • Enter starts a new line. Ctrl + Enter sends the reply.
  • The default dialogue length went from 300 to 800 characters, and the limit you can set went from 1,000 to 2,000.
  • The limit you pick now applies every time, in regular dialogue and festival dialogue.
In GMCMMax Dialogue Lengthdefault 800, up to 2,000
New

Vietnamese and a language choice

AliveNpcs now fully supports Vietnamese, with fonts and Unicode rendering so accented characters show up correctly across the game.

  • Our Vietnamese friends can now flirt with Robin in their own language.
  • A new language setting has three choices: follow Stardew Valley's language, use Vietnamese or type in the language you want for AI replies.
  • The translations for the mod and the official EC pack are better.
  • Long text gets shortened more carefully. Unicode characters are kept, and words aren't cut in half whenever that can be avoided.
  • If you have any trouble playing AliveNpcs in your language, reach out to me. I'll look for a fix so everyone can enjoy the mod in their own language.

For mod creators

Integrations and the public API

The public API grew, and other mods can now generate content with the AI provider the player already set up.

  • The API can turn NPCs on or off across every AliveNpcs system.
  • Partner mods can look up the available NPCs and the content generators AliveNpcs provides.
  • Other mods can generate dialogue, letters, interactions, social moments, gossip analysis and free-form content with the player's AI provider.
  • The API also controls independent shops, character customizations, player character sheets and Experimental Content packs.
  • Companion mods can read and update the player's character sheet, check the NPCs' original values and restore customizations more safely.
  • 1.5.0 announced detailed documentation for release soon.

Before you update

  • Enmity and expulsion arcs are experimental and off by default.
  • You don't need any optional integration to use AliveNpcs. Beach Episode, the Personality Manager and the multiplayer companion are all optional.
  • Your previous settings keep working wherever possible, including the old EC mode names and the old personality file.

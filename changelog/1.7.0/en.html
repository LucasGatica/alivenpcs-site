Keep in Touch

in development

A mid-season update packed with features and fixes. The phone gets more apps, the Messenger comes alive, the diary becomes a scrapbook with fan art of the villagers by independent artists, and AliveNpcs tries again on its own when Gemini is overloaded.

New

More apps on the phone

  • Settings, Junimogotchi and More are on the phone too, each with a layout made for its screen.
  • Pomodoro (phone only): pick your focus and break minutes, press Start focus and put the phone away. It counts real time, even with the game paused, and rings when the time is up.
  • To-do (phone only): your own to-do list, kept per save. Tick off what you've done and use Clear done tasks to sweep it away.
New

A livelier Messenger

  • Selfies: a villager who texts you first can send a selfie from wherever they are, with a comment on the place.
  • Real texts: replies come short, the way people really text, in one or two sentences.
  • They really seem to type: after the AI answers, the villager keeps “typing” a moment longer, and the text appears bit by bit.
  • Birthdays: on a villager's birthday their chat moves to the top with a cake, and that day's reply is special. They react to you remembering, or forgetting.
  • After 1 am: the villager notices the hour and, depending on who they are, may answer sleepy, worried or annoyed. You may even lose some friendship.
  • Not everyone has a phone: Linus, Krobus, the Wizard, Leo, Birdie, Vincent and Jas show greyed out, with the reason why. Vincent, for one, is grounded indefinitely.
  • The list has two tabs: Contacts, with everyone, and Chats, with only the villagers you've already texted.
In the settingsAppsa new page with the apps' options. Messages per day: 5 by default, up to 15.
New

The diary becomes a scrapbook

The Player Diary can now show fan art of the villagers, drawn by independent artists who gave permission for it.

  • When an entry mentions a villager, one of their pictures is taped beside it like a polaroid, with the artist's name. Each picture is pasted only once and stays there for good.
  • Click a picture to see it large, with a link to the artist's portfolio.
  • Album: a tab at the foot of the diary with every picture, by villager, with silhouettes for the ones you haven't found yet and credits for every artist.
  • Seasonal cover: the diary opens closed, and its cover, with an artist's art, turns to a title page that credits them. In a hurry? Tick Skip the opening at the foot of the diary.
  • Export PDF: the whole diary, with cover, pages and credits, becomes a PDF in the mod's folder, with clickable links to the artists.
  • Diary pages are now numbered.
  • The first pictures are by Andy Illustra, ACarts and bolopuffs. Meet the illustrators.
  • The pictures belong to their artists and may not be used outside AliveNpcs.
In the settingsDiary scrapbookunder Gossip & News. You can turn it off, and the Diary opening animation too.
New

Gossip about the villagers, not just you

The nightly gossip no longer has to be about you.

  • Balanced (the new default): the village gossips about the villagers first, using what they did among themselves and what they told you about each other. You only become the talk of the town when you do something out of the ordinary.
  • Villagers only: the gossip is always about the villagers, and they meet each other a little more often each day.
  • Normal: the gossip comes from your day, as before.
  • Your diary doesn't change: it's still about your own day.
In the settingsGossip About Youunder Gossip & News.
New

Watering a villager

  • Pour your watering can over a villager and they come over to ask why. Out of summer they're wet and annoyed, and in winter the water is freezing. In summer, depending on who they are, they may even enjoy it.
  • Villagers who only watched find it funny and remember it. It counts for a charged watering can too, on every tile it waters.
Improved

Gemini overloaded? It tries again on its own

  • When Gemini says it's overloaded, AliveNpcs sends the request again right away, up to 5 times. You no longer have to click the villager again.
  • Meanwhile, the window shows which try it's on, for example 2/5.

The phone

New

Seen and Heard: they notice you

When you do something near a villager, they may see or hear it. It becomes one of their memories and affects how they feel. Depending on what it was, they walk over to comment right away. Later they may bring it up in other conversations or spread it as gossip at night.

What they notice

Giving a gift may walk over

The person receiving it and anyone nearby remember the item, whether they liked it and whether it was their birthday. The person who got it only walks over to thank you if they didn't dislike or hate the gift.

Eating or drinking drinks: may walk over

Ordinary food is only remembered. Alcohol can make someone walk over, and each villager reacts in their own way.

Setting off bombs may walk over

The noise carries through walls and can wake up anyone who was asleep.

Following someone closely walks over

Walk behind a villager for 3 seconds and a ! appears. Keep going for 3 more and they turn around, come after you and tell you to stop.

Fishing nearby may fish with you

See Fishing together.

Digging through trash may walk over

They know whose trash can it was, and only the owner comes over to confront you.

Tool or weapon may walk over

Hitting someone (or only just missing) with an axe, pickaxe, hoe, watering can or weapon.

Slingshot may walk over

Hitting someone or narrowly missing them.

Bedroom door may walk over

Trying to open the bedroom of someone you barely know (under 2) or opening the door for the first time.

Walking into someone's bedroom may walk over

They notice how long you stayed inside, and the owner of the room may come over to talk to you.

Dating or marriage proposal may walk over

With a bouquet, a Mermaid's Pendant or through dialogue. They remember whether the answer was yes or no. Someone already dating or married to you comes to confront you.

Talking with someone only remembers

Anyone nearby sees you two talking. What was said is only overheard if the conversation wasn't marked as secret.

Walking around town only remembers

Going in and out of homes and shops, or moving from one area of the map to another.

Shopping only remembers

The shopkeeper and anyone in the shop remember what you bought on that visit.

Passing out away from home only remembers

At 2 AM or from exhaustion. The day ends there, but anyone who saw it may bring it up in the following days.

Walking by hurt or exhausted comes to help

See Friends who look after you.

What they feel

  • A small thing is only remembered. Doing it again in front of the same person, or doing something serious, is what makes them walk over. There are four feelings, each with three levels:
Confrontation
suspicious, curiousgenuinely upset, wants an explanationfurious, won't have forgotten by tomorrow
Amusement
pokes a little funlaughs in your face, no malicethe funniest thing all week
Fondness
pleased, wants to say something nicelikes having you aroundtouched, “this is what makes someone family”
Romance
flustered, can't leave it unsaidopenly taken with you, past pretendinghead over heels, wants you to hear it from them
  • Each villager reads the same scene their own way. Shane laughs at your drinking, Gus buys you a beer after a few rounds and Penny gets upset if you drink near a child.
  • A gift the person hated earns no fondness at all. And if your partner sees you asking someone else out, they come over to confront you.
  • The third time someone sees you trying to open their bedroom door, it's not just curiosity anymore.
  • Each level goes up at most once a day, and each feeling has a daily limit per villager. Strong anger and romance partly carry over to the next day.

How they walk over

  • A ! appears above their head and the villager walks quickly to your side. No teleporting.
  • Their line is generated while they walk. If they arrive first, they wait beside you until it's ready.
  • The line is about what happened, in the tone of what they're feeling. They don't change the subject or bring up old gossip instead.
  • Only the villager with the best reason comes over. Anyone else who also felt strongly, or wasn't sure of what they saw, brings it up the next time you talk.
  • If you talk to someone who was about to come over, they bring it up in that conversation.
  • When the conversation ends, including your replies, they walk back to where they were and pick up their routine.
  • If you save, sleep, change maps or enter an event, the approach is canceled and they go back to normal.

How they remember

  • Memories are written from the villager's own point of view, and only as sure as they are. Someone right next to you “clearly saw” it, someone further away “thinks they saw” it, and an explosion is heard, not seen.
  • Each memory fades at its own pace. An explosion or someone getting hit sticks around for weeks. Walking past someone lasts about a day. When too much piles up, the most forgettable ones go first.
  • What they saw comes up later in normal conversations and can make it into the evening gossip.
  • You can see what each villager feels about you today in the villager diary.

Compatibility

  • SpaceCore is not required.
  • Villagers controlled by another mod, like PelicanCompanions companions, are left alone.
  • If a game update breaks the detection of one action, only that action stops working. The rest of the game and the mod carry on as normal.

New

Nothing gets lost when the AI fails

An overloaded provider, or an answer that took too long or came back unreadable: before, you lost the day or the conversation. Now there's a Retry button. You'll never lose a diary entry to provider hiccups again.

Diary and gossip

  • If the AI fails during the night, you get a warning in chat. The Player Diary and the Gossip Diary get a button that runs the analysis again, with the same conversations from that day.
  • The button turns orange while the day's diary or gossip is missing. If it was already generated, it just tells you so.
  • When it works, the AI's entry replaces the short notes left on the night it failed.
  • Only the latest day can be retried, and the button keeps working after you quit and come back to the game.
  • In multiplayer, each player retries their own diary. Only the host can retry the village gossip.

Conversations

  • If the AI fails to answer you, the conversation no longer closes. The reply window stays open, explains what happened and shows Try again, Edit and End.
  • Try again sends your reply again, exactly as you wrote it, with the action you chose.
  • Before, a failure from the second turn on ended the conversation, and you couldn't talk to that villager again that day.
  • This also covers bonus actions, invitations, relationship proposals and festival actions.
New

Group conversations

When you arrive somewhere two villagers are standing together, they may call out “Hey, (your name)!”.

  • One of them walks over with the ! while the other calls out to you. The conversation opens in its own menu, with both villagers and you.
  • They answer you and each other. Each one has their own friendship change.
  • You can close the conversation even while they're answering. Leaving without saying anything is remembered too.
  • Afterwards both of them remember talking with you and with the other villager.

New

NVIDIA NIM, a new AI provider

Besides Gemini and OpenAI-compatible servers, you can now use NVIDIA NIM. The API keys are free at build.nvidia.com.

  • You can add up to 20 keys (3 by default). The mod takes turns with them and, when one hits its limit, moves to the next one on its own and tells you.
  • A key that gets rejected is set aside until you close the game.
  • Create each key with a different NVIDIA account: keys from the same account share the same limit.
  • The default model is nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b, and you can switch to another one.
  • Switching providers keeps your Gemini and custom server settings.

New

Friends who look after you

If you walk near someone (within 8 tiles) with your health or energy at 30% or less, how they react depends on your friendship.

  • 4 or more: the villager comes over, says something and gives you food. Many villagers have a food of their own; everyone else gives a simple snack. At most 1 helping per day, counting everyone.
  • 2 or 3: they only comment, also once a day.
Improved

Scenes without teleporting

Scene events used to be cutscenes that moved you and the villagers somewhere else. Now, with the default style, a scene only happens if the villagers are actually there on their own schedule.

  • With 1 villager: they notice you, call out, walk over and talk. Then they go back to what they were doing.
  • With 2 or more: you overhear them chatting in speech bubbles nearby. Nothing locks and no menu opens, so you can keep doing what you were doing.
  • With AI, the lines are created from a short description of the moment, in each character's own voice. Without AI, the written lines are used.
  • If you talk to a villager who was getting ready to walk over to you, they don't come over afterwards to start a second conversation.
  • The two closing lines that repeated across 100 scenes are gone, in every language.
  • Fixed three scenes where a villager talked to themselves (Evelyn and the flour on Alex's face, Gus and Pam's empty mug, Alex calling George “Grandpa”).

You can pick the style

These options used to live only in config.json. Now they are in the settings, on the Village Life page, under Everyday scenes:

  • Natural (default): villagers walk over to talk with you or talk among themselves.
  • Ambient only: you only overhear the conversations. Nobody walks over to you.
  • Cinematic: the classic cutscenes, like before.

You can also turn scenes on or off and choose how many happen per day (0 to 5).

Good to know

  • In multiplayer, walk-up scenes and overheard conversations only happen for the host. Other players don't get the cutscenes unless they pick Cinematic.
  • Experimental mode intros and scenes from Experimental Content packs still play as cutscenes. Packs can switch to the new format if they want.

For pack creators

New triggers from a single JSON file

A content pack can include an alive-trigger.json file and add new reactions to Seen and Heard, without writing code.

  • When: a game condition (weather, time of day, items in your inventory and so on) checked near villagers.
  • On: changes how villagers react to something they already notice, like a gift, a bomb, walking into a bedroom or talking with someone.
  • You can filter by the details of each action: whether the drink was alcoholic, whose trash can or bedroom it was, whether the gift was for your partner or a birthday, and which shop the purchase was in.
  • A trigger with a reaction or an item sends the villager straight to you. A trigger with only a summary leaves it up to their feelings.
  • You can filter by villager and by hearts, limit how many times a day it fires, keep it as a memory only, and have the villager give you an item.
  • Gifts, food and purchases now store the item ID, so triggers work in any game language.
  • Up to 50 triggers per pack. The pack must require AliveNpcs 1.7.0 in its manifest. The full format is in ALIVE_TRIGGERS.md.

Apps for the computer

Other mods can put their own apps in the farm computer's Apps folder, through IAliveNpcsComputerApi. The step-by-step guide is in COMPUTER_APPS.md.

Test commands in the SMAPI console

alive_trigger lint [pack]Re-reads the file and explains each trigger: what was rejected, what has no effect and whether the condition holds right now.
alive_trigger reloadApplies the edited pack without restarting the game.
alive_trigger listShows the active triggers and how many times they fired today.
alive_sh fire <event>Simulates an action near villagers and shows who saw it and who is walking over.
alive_sh feelings [npc]Shows what each villager feels about you today and how close they are to walking over.
alive_group_chatForces a group conversation.

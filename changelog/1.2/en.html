1.2.0

Arcs with endings, letters with gifts

Mar 27, 2026

Story arcs were rebuilt, letters bring gifts and the mod accepts several Gemini keys.

New

Story arcs with objectives

Each story arc now has 3 to 5 objectives in a row, like talking to someone or bringing an item. The mod tracks progress on its own from the villagers' replies.

  • The arcs menu shows how many objectives you have done, for example 2/3.
  • Each arc gets a colored label: [READY!], [CONCLUDED] or [EXPIRED].
  • Once every objective is done, the Conclude Arc! button shows up in the menu.

Good to know

  • Only one new arc is detected per week, so the valley doesn't fill up with stories all at once.
  • An arc that goes 2 or more seasons without progress expires on its own.
In GMCMArc Expiry (seasons)default 2
New

Arc endings become a scene

When you conclude an arc, a scene in the style of heart events shows how it ends. The AI writes the dialogue between the villagers involved.

  • The text appears letter by letter, like on a typewriter.
  • The villagers' portraits change emotion: neutral, happy, sad, angry and in love.
  • The scene switches between speakers, fades in and out, and has dramatic music and a setting that fits the story.
  • After the ending, the villagers involved and the ones outside it talk about the outcome for 3 days.
  • The outcome becomes part of your save's official story. Every villager remembers it in future conversations, so everyone tells the same story.
  • The outcome also goes into the village gossip.
In GMCMArc Conclusion Sceneon by default
In GMCMPost-Arc Dialogue (days)default 3
New

The talk after a heart event

The first conversation after a heart event now has a special line. The villager reacts to what you went through together without retelling the scene.

  • It works with all 12 villagers you can date. Their heart event IDs were fixed.
  • The mod keeps track of which heart events you have seen, from one conversation to the next.
  • Memories of these events in conversations follow your friendship level and only show up from 4.
New

Multiple Gemini keys

You can now add up to 20 Gemini API keys. When a key hits its daily quota, the mod moves to the next one on its own, without interrupting the game.

  • During the session, the mod keeps track of which keys hit the limit and which ones look invalid.
  • An in-game message tells you when the mod switches keys and how the quota is doing.
In GMCM➕ Number of Keysdefault 3, from 1 to 20. Sets how many key fields show up.
Improved

Letters with gifts

Villagers now send a gift along with their letters. The mod picks an item the villager loves or likes on its own.

  • The gift has a sell price limit: up to 500g for an item the villager likes and up to 750g for an item they love.
  • Rare or progression items, like the Prismatic Shard, are on a block list and never go in letters.
  • Each villager waits a while before sending another letter. The default is 7 days.
  • You can get more than one letter a day if you raise the limit.
  • Letters now take into account how the villager feels and the village gossip.
  • Villagers from other mods can send gifts too.

Improved

AliveNpcs Hub and the game menu

The AliveNpcs Hub (F9) has new tabs and a button to turn the AI on and off. You can now also open the Hub from the game's own menu.

  • New tab: Character Sheet.
  • The Personalities tab shows up when a companion mod, like the Personality Editor, is installed.
  • The AI button turns the AI features on and off. With the AI off, villagers use the game's normal lines.
  • The Hub can show your token usage.
  • The game menu has a new AliveNpcs Hub button next to the inventory tab. It looks like Stardew Valley's brown tabs and opens the Hub.
  • The arcs menu shows objective progress, the colored labels and the conclude button. See Story arcs with objectives.
In the HubEnable AI featureson by default
In GMCMShow Token Usageoff by default. Shows token usage at the bottom of the Hub.
Improved

Longer conversations

A conversation with a villager can now have up to 15 turns. The old maximum was 3.

  • The Character Sheet has a fourth field: your character's appearance, like pronouns, hair color and clothing style.
  • When a villager has no personality of their own in AliveNpcs, the AI uses one of their original lines as a style guide.
  • A new option limits how many friendship points one conversation can give.
In GMCMTurns per Conversationdefault 1, up to 15
In GMCMMax Points per Interactiondefault 100
Improved

Kent is away in Year 1

In the first year, Kent is away from the valley. His family can talk about him with longing, and he stays out of arcs and gossip.

  • Jodi, Sam and Vincent can mention Kent with longing.
  • Jodi and Sam have different personalities in Year 1 and from Year 2 on.
  • Gossip leaves out interactions with villagers who are away, before and after the day's analysis.
Fixes

More stability

The mod handles Gemini failures and replies generated in the background better.

  • If every key runs out of quota, villagers go back to the game's normal lines and a message tells you why.
  • The mod sorts Gemini errors by type: missing key, invalid key, usage limit, server error, timeout and network failure.
  • Lines generated in the background now reach the game through a safe queue.
  • Messages from calls made in the background now show up in the game.
  • The gossip that shows up in the chat in the morning uses the gossip summary when the message comes back empty.
For mod creators

API for other mods

AliveNpcs now has a public API for companion mods, like the Personality Editor.

  • Custom personalities can be loaded from external JSON files.
  • With the companion mod installed, the Hub gets the Personalities tab.
GetDefaultPersonality()Returns a villager's default personality.
HasCustomPersonality()Tells whether a villager has a custom personality.
